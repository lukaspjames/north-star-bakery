About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to bring handmade baked goods and a welcoming neighborhood experience to the local community.

We believe a bakery can be more than a place to buy bread. It can be a place where neighbors, families, commuters, and local organizations connect.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on quality ingredients, trusted suppliers, seasonal products, and thoughtful preparation.
